Über das Spiel

Iron Front: Isonzo ist ein 2D-Taktikspiel an der Isonzofront des Ersten Weltkriegs. Deine Soldaten rücken selbstständig vor, suchen Deckung, erwidern das Feuer, werden niedergehalten und verlieren die Nerven. Du entscheidest, welcher Trupp wann und wohin geht, und setzt knappe Unterstützung im richtigen Moment ein.

About the game

Iron Front: Isonzo is a 2D tactics game set on the Isonzo front of the First World War. Your soldiers advance on their own, seek cover, return fire, get pinned down and lose their nerve. You decide which squad goes where and when, and spend scarce support at the right moment.

  • Italy or Austria-Hungary: both sides are playable, across karst, valley, alpine and forest battlefields.
  • Rifle squads, machine-gun teams, storm troops, engineers, mountain troops and officers with their own roles.
  • Suppression, cover, morale and terrain decide whether an assault breaks through.
  • Roguelite campaign: a front map that shifts back and forth, a regiment with veterans and lasting losses.
  • The Hall of Remembrance honours your regiments and their fallen.
  • Plays fully offline. No in-app purchases; ads are optional rewards only.

The tone is serious and documentary. The war is not glorified. Available in 26 languages.
