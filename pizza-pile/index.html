About the game

Tap, stack, amaze! In Pizza Pile, toppings slide across your pizza – tap at the right moment to drop them. Whatever hangs over gets cut off: your pizza gets narrower and your tower grows ever higher. Hit it exactly for a perfect drop – and every perfect makes your pizza grow back a little.

  • One finger is all you need: perfect hits, combos, golden toppings, turbo layers and pizza fever.
  • From Peppo's cozy pizzeria over the rooftops into space, to the moon, Mars, the sun and a black hole.
  • 65 crazy outfits, 20 topping sets, 26 plates, 15 decorations and a recipe book with 40 recipes.
  • Daily challenge, daily gifts, missions, a lucky wheel, the Pizza Pass and seasonal events.
  • Game modes: time attack (60 seconds) and zen.
  • Google Play Games: leaderboards, achievements and cloud save.
